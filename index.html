A few things, before you brew.

How do I order?

Choose a tea, pack size, and quantity. Add it to your bag, then send your order on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, your delivery address, and the total before placing the order.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is Rs 450 around Kandy and along the A1 road towards Colombo. Orders normally arrive within 3–7 days. We confirm your address and delivery date on WhatsApp. For other locations, please contact us first.

What is the return policy?

Tea orders are generally not returnable. As a one-time exception, a regular customer with more than 10 completed purchases may request one return for an unopened pack under 500g, subject to confirmation on WhatsApp.
